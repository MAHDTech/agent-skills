class="theme-doc-markdown markdown">\<header\>\<h1\>Maincloud</h1></header><p>Maincloud is SpacetimeDB&#x27;s fully managed serverless platform. It handles infrastructure, scaling, replication, and backups so you can focus on building your application. Maincloud scales to zero when your database is idle, so you only pay for what you use.</p>
<p>For pricing details, see the <a href="https://spacetimedb.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">pricing page</a>.</p>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="prerequisites">Prerequisites<a href="#prerequisites" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Prerequisites" title="Direct link to Prerequisites" translate="no">​</a></h2>
\<ol\>
<li class="">Install the SpacetimeDB CLI: <a href="https://spacetimedb.com/install" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">Install SpacetimeDB</a></li>
<li class="">Log in to link your CLI identity with your web account:</li>
</ol>
<pre tabindex="0" class="codeBlockStandalone_LlrK thin-scrollbar codeBlockContainer_hFbQ theme-code-block" style="--prism-color:#bfc7d5;--prism-background-color:#292d3e"><code class="codeBlockLines_JqGU"><span class="line"><span style="color:#50FA7B">spacetime</span><span style="color:#F1FA8C"> login</span></span></code></pre>
<p>This opens a browser window where you sign in with your GitHub or Google account. Once authenticated, your CLI identity is linked to your Maincloud account, and any databases you publish will appear on the web dashboard.</p>
<div class="theme-admonition theme-admonition-tip admonition_Ph9R alert alert--success"><div class="admonitionHeading_kG9t"><span class="admonitionIcon_VA_f"><svg viewBox="0 0 12 16"><path fill-rule="evenodd" d="M6.5 0C3.48 0 1 2.19 1 5c0 .92.55 2.25 1 3 1.34 2.25 1.78 2.78 2 4v1h5v-1c.22-1.22.66-1.75 2-4 .45-.75 1-2.08 1-3 0-2.81-2.48-5-5.5-5zm3.64 7.48c-.25.44-.47.8-.67 1.11-.86 1.41-1.25 2.06-1.45 3.23-.02.05-.02.11-.02.17H5c0-.06 0-.13-.02-.17-.2-1.17-.59-1.83-1.45-3.23-.2-.31-.42-.67-.67-1.11C2.44 6.78 2 5.65 2 5c0-2.2 2.02-4 4.5-4 1.22 0 2.36.42 3.22 1.19C10.55 2.94 11 3.94 11 5c0 .66-.44 1.78-.86 2.48zM4 14h5c-.23 1.14-1.3 2-2.5 2s-2.27-.86-2.5-2z"></path></svg></span>tip</div><div class="admonitionContent_STxx"><p>If you previously published a database without logging in first, your CLI identity will not be linked to your web account. Run <code>spacetime logout</code> followed by <code>spacetime login</code> to re-authenticate.</p></div></div>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="publishing-to-maincloud">Publishing to Maincloud<a href="#publishing-to-maincloud" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Publishing to Maincloud" title="Direct link to Publishing to Maincloud" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p>After creating your module (see <a class="" href="/docs/">Getting Started</a>), publish it to Maincloud:</p>
<pre tabindex="0" class="codeBlockStandalone_LlrK thin-scrollbar codeBlockContainer_hFbQ theme-code-block" style="--prism-color:#bfc7d5;--prism-background-color:#292d3e"><code class="codeBlockLines_JqGU"><span class="line"><span style="color:#50FA7B">spacetime</span><span style="color:#F1FA8C"> publish</span><span style="color:#F1FA8C"> my-database</span><span style="color:#BD93F9"> --server</span><span style="color:#F1FA8C"> maincloud</span></span></code></pre>
<p>SpacetimeDB compiles your module, uploads it, runs your <code>init</code> reducer (if defined), and outputs the database identity. Save this identity for administrative tasks.</p>
<p>To update an existing module, run the same command. SpacetimeDB hot-swaps the module code without disconnecting clients. See <a class="" href="/docs/databases/automatic-migrations">Automatic Migrations</a> for details on schema changes during updates.</p>
<p>To clear all data and start fresh:</p>

<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="viewing-your-database-on-the-web-dashboard">Viewing Your Database on the Web Dashboard<a href="#viewing-your-database-on-the-web-dashboard" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Viewing Your Database on the Web Dashboard" title="Direct link to Viewing Your Database on the Web Dashboard" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p>After publishing, you can manage your database through the web dashboard at <a href="https://spacetimedb.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">spacetimedb.com</a>.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="finding-your-database">Finding your database<a href="#finding-your-database" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Finding your database" title="Direct link to Finding your database" translate="no">​</a></h3>
<p>There are two ways to navigate to your database:</p>
\<ol\>
<li class=""><strong>Direct URL</strong>: Go to <code>https://spacetimedb.com/my-database</code> or <code>https://spacetimedb.com/@my-username/my-database</code> (replacing <code>my-database</code> with your database name).</li>
<li class=""><strong>Profile page</strong>: Click your profile picture in the top-right corner of <a href="https://spacetimedb.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">spacetimedb.com</a> and select "My profile". All of your published databases are listed there. You can also navigate directly to <code>https://spacetimedb.com/@your-username</code>.</li>
</ol>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="dashboard-features">Dashboard features<a href="#dashboard-features" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Dashboard features" title="Direct link to Dashboard features" translate="no">​</a></h3>
<p>The database dashboard gives you access to:</p>
\<ul\>
<li class=""><strong>Overview</strong>: View your database identity, name, status (Running or Paused), table and reducer counts, and energy usage. The overview also shows stats for the past 24 hours including CCU, rows per table, and transactions.</li>
<li class=""><strong>Usage breakdown</strong>: See this month&#x27;s energy consumption broken down by bytes scanned, bytes written, index seeks, CPU instructions, bandwidth, and table storage.</li>
<li class=""><strong>Logs</strong>: View your module&#x27;s log output in real time.</li>
<li class=""><strong>SQL console</strong>: Run ad-hoc SQL queries against your database.</li>
<li class=""><strong>SpacetimeAuth</strong>: Enable and configure the built-in authentication provider (see <a class="" href="/docs/core-concepts/authentication/spacetimeauth/">SpacetimeAuth</a>).</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="database-lifecycle">Database Lifecycle<a href="#database-lifecycle" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Database Lifecycle" title="Direct link to Database Lifecycle" translate="no">​</a></h2>
<p>Maincloud databases have two states:</p>
\<ul\>
<li class=""><strong>Running</strong> (green dot on dashboard): The database is actively serving requests. Any client connection, reducer call, or dashboard visit will keep it in this state.</li>
<li class=""><strong>Paused</strong> (pause icon on dashboard): The database is suspended. All data is preserved, but the database is not serving requests and does not consume energy.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="automatic-suspension-free-tier">Automatic suspension (Free tier)<a href="#automatic-suspension-free-tier" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Automatic suspension (Free tier)" title="Direct link to Automatic suspension (Free tier)" translate="no">​</a></h3>
<p>On the Free tier, Maincloud automatically pauses databases after a period of inactivity (no client connections, no reducer calls). A paused database resumes automatically when it receives a connection or request. Startup time is typically less than one second.</p>
<p>On the Pro and Team tiers, databases are never automatically suspended as long as you have pay-as-you-go enabled or have not exceeded your self-set spending limit. If you want to ensure your database is always available, upgrade to the <a href="https://spacetimedb.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">Pro or Team tier</a>.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Y_NL" id="manual-pause-and-resume">Manual pause and resume<a href="#manual-pause-and-resume" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Manual pause and resume" title="Direct link to Manual pause and resume" translate="no">​</a></h3>
<p>You can manually pause and resume your database from the web dashboard:</p>
\<ol\>
<li class="">Navigate to your database on <a href="https://spacetimedb.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="">spacetimedb.com</a>.</li>
<li class="">In the left sidebar, check the <strong>Status</strong> field to see if your database is Running or Paused.</li>
<li class="">Click <strong>Pause Database</strong> to suspend the database, or <strong>Start Database</strong> to resume it.</li>
</ol>
<p>Pausing a database stops all energy usage for that database. This is useful if you want to keep your data but are not actively using the database.</p>
