# Core Concepts

Tauri has a variety of topics that are considered to be core concepts,
things any developer should be aware of when developing their
applications. Here’s a variety of topics that you should get more
intimately familiar with if you want to get the most out of the
framework.

[Tauri Architecture](https://v2.tauri.app/concept/architecture/)Architecture and ecosystem.

[Inter-Process Communication
(IPC)](https://v2.tauri.app/concept/inter-process-communication/)The inner workings on the
IPC.

[Security](https://v2.tauri.app/security/)How Tauri enforces security practices.

[Process Model](https://v2.tauri.app/concept/process-model/)Which processes Tauri manages
and why.

[App Size](https://v2.tauri.app/concept/size/)How to make your app as small as possible.
