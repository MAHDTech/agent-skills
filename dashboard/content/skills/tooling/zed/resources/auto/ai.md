## Full control in full-speed

Zed offers a powerful platform for interacting with agents, bringing
many options to connect with them and control their behavior.

![Hosted by
Zed](https://zed.dev/cdn-cgi/image/width=2048,quality=75,format=auto/img/agentic/hosted.webp)

[Learn More →](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/overview)

Hosted by Zed

Use the best models like Claude Opus and GPT 5.4 with simple pricing and
no API keys to manage.

![Run
locally](https://zed.dev/cdn-cgi/image/width=2048,quality=75,format=auto/img/agentic/run-locally.webp)

[Learn More →](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/llm-providers#ollama)

Run locally

Connect models through Ollama and keep everything on your hardware.
Nothing leaves your machine.

![Bring your own
keys](https://zed.dev/cdn-cgi/image/width=2048,quality=75,format=auto/img/agentic/api-keys.webp)

[Learn More →](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/llm-providers#use-your-own-keys)

Bring your own keys

Plug in API keys from any provider. Use your existing accounts, your
existing budgets.

Native speed

Written in Rust, rendered at 120fps. When your agent edits 50 files, you
see every change as it happens.

[Learn about Zed→](https://zed.dev/blog/videogame)

Fine-grain tool control

A granular settings system for configuring permissions for what tools
your agent has access to.

[View Tool Permissions→](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/tool-permissions)

Open source, open protocol, open model

Zed, Zeta, and ACP are all open source. The editor, the model, and the
protocol, built in public.

[Explore ACP→](https://zed.dev/acp)
