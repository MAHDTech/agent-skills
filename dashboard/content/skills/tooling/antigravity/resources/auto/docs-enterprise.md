<p>Review the following solutions for common sign-in and license issues:</p>
\<ul\>
\<li\><strong>No licenses appear during setup</strong>: Licenses are assigned by your organization’s Google Cloud administrator. If the License Selector is empty, contact your administrator to ensure your account has been granted access to a Gemini Enterprise Standard or Plus license.</li>
\<li\><strong>Missing BYOID sign-in option</strong>: Ensure you are running the latest release of <strong><a href="/download">Antigravity 2.0</a></strong>, the <strong><a href="/docs/cli/install">Antigravity CLI</a></strong>, or your <strong><a href="/docs/ide/extensions">IDE extension</a></strong>, as enterprise authentication and BYOID support are included natively in all recent releases.</li>
</ul>
