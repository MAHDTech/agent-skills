# Browser Recordings[link](#browser-recordings)

Every time the browser subagent actuates on the Browser, it may choose
to generate a recording of the agent’s actions for your review. You can
view this playback, if it is available, at the bottom of the Browser
step UI.

![Browser Recording Capture
Tool](https://antigravity.google/assets/image/docs/artifacts/browser-recording-capture.png)

All browser recordings are also saved as a recording artifact for your
review. This view loops through the browser agent’s actions.

![Browser Recording
Artifact](https://antigravity.google/assets/image/docs/artifacts/browser-recording-artifact.png)
